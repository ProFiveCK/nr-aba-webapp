# Government Leave Package 4 — other leave and assisted cases

Implemented locally on `codex/employee-account-foundation`. Review at **http://localhost:8081** using the isolated `ron-leave-review` Docker stack. The stack retains synthetic data between restarts; SMTP, AI and unattended government accrual jobs remain disabled. Nothing is pushed, deployed to production, sent to Payroll or emailed.

## Review locations

- `/review/employee` → **My Leave**: the ordinary certified-balance form remains, followed by **Event leave and assisted cases**. Select an event type, dates, personnel/event reference, explanation and documents. Submission requests review; it does not grant leave or assign an annual balance.
- `/review/hr` → **Staff → Government workflow**: load an employee, then use **HR assisted application** for an employee who cannot submit themselves, financial/separation cases or reviewed attendance. The employee request/authority reference is mandatory; applicant and entering officer are distinct audit facts. Inactive employees can be selected for a sourced separation case.
- Open a government application to **Prepare case determination**, attach further evidence before HR verification, review dated salary segments and track the required offices. A different central HR verifier must verify the preparer's facts. Minister escalation has a separate dated department-specific Minister appointment and statutory grant before Chief Secretary final processing.
- **Case follow-up register** lists open/completed/reopened/non-completion tasks in bounded pages of 50, optionally for the loaded employee. Dated overdue tasks are highlighted. **Review follow-up case** opens the corresponding application; actual receipts/Salary Unit references are required where applicable. Refresh retrieves the latest register.
- On an approved application, **Start controlled amendment** requests cancellation or shortening after early return. On a pending application, **Continue with fresh route approvals** atomically cancels/releases the old request and submits a linked replacement against current placement and offices. All approvals are required again; old decisions/documents remain in history.

## Delivered rules

| Case | Enforced behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Maternity | Twelve calendar weeks, capped by verified employment cessation. Verified female-employee eligibility and pregnancy count; certificate details and fitness to continue within six weeks of expected delivery. First four pregnancies use full salary after qualification, fifth onward half salary. Mid-leave six-month qualification requires a split salary schedule; the conflicting fifth-pregnancy/full-pay combination requires a signed resolution selecting the permissible half/full treatment. Three-month notice uses calendar months; a late application needs an authority reference and explicit Chief Secretary discretionary confirmation. Return-contact task due four weeks before leave ends; completion records the same/equivalent position and retained benefits. |
| Paternity | Two calendar weeks beginning at the recorded birth/adoption event, subject to cessation. Verified male-employee eligibility, parenthood and certificate/adoption evidence. Adoption child must be under twelve months. Six-month qualification governs dated pay, including a mid-leave split. Late notice requires Chief Secretary discretion. Birth event creates a Births Register follow-up. |
| Adoption | Up to twelve calendar weeks, beginning at adoption even for a later application, subject to cessation. Verified female-employee eligibility, certified adoption order, child under twelve months and explicit exclusion of spouse's child/step-child. Six-month service/pay rule and split when reached during leave. |
| Extended medical | Outside the ordinary ten-day pool. Practitioner/complete-period evidence and reconciliation of ordinary medical usage are mandatory. Chief Secretary tier is at most three calendar months at full salary. The further Minister tier requires a linked approved complete three-month extension that has expired, is at most twelve calendar months, and has explicit full/partial/zero salary segments. Relevant Secretary and Minister decisions are retained separately from Chief Secretary final processing. |
| Official travel | Event outside the Republic with verified official purpose, documents and actual Gazette references; full salary. Any allowance needs a signed Minister-rate amount/reference. Trip outcome task records completion or non-completion. Non-completion requires actual payment reference/paid amount and signed full/pro-rata recovery, bounded by approved/paid allowance. Server derives the five-working-day deadline from the approved calendar and certified schedule/roster. Actual repayment to HOD is a separate receipt task. Return/attendance consequences require review; no automatic disciplinary penalty. Temporary employees, including separately designated temporary interns, retain official-travel eligibility. |
| Witness for the Republic | Verified court attendance, prompt HOD notification and full salary; no annual pool. Follow-up records actual witness-fee remittance and explicitly excludes travelling allowances/expenses. |
| Other witness | Unpaid court attendance; explicit signed resolution of the conflicting policy authority wording and deciding-authority reference. HOD notification alone cannot grant it. Kept separate from permanent-only LWOP; it does not automatically create an LWOP service pause. Unaddressed temporary coverage remains blocked pending an approved policy variation. |
| LWOP | Permanent only, genuine/justified purpose, no other accessible leave, relevant Secretary consent and Chief Secretary grant. Zero salary. More than twelve calendar months needs relevant study or exceptional circumstances, signed authority and explicit Chief Secretary discretionary confirmation. Cannot run past a verified appointment end. Final grant records an immutable qualifying-service exclusion without breaking continuity; altered service context blocks affected jobs/requests pending reconciliation. Return task is separate. |
| Teacher recreation | Verified teacher appointment, Chief Secretary discretionary period/unit and signed Education processing order. Full salary; timesheet follow-up. No ordinary Recreation balance or accrual formula is invented. Temporary teacher Recreation retains the twelve-month service gate. Nonstandard schedules need verified roster conversions. |
| Long service | Certified credited service: twenty working days from five to under eight years, forty from eight to under ten. Contract employees included. Only qualifying separation/estate events or Chief Secretary encashment; unrestricted taking leave is unavailable. Signed prior usage/payouts, unit conversion, LSL-to-furlough treatment and monetary valuation are required. |
| Furlough | Sixty units at ten credited years plus nine per completed additional year; future milestones cannot finance a current commitment. Taking leave requires HOD and Chief Secretary decisions and units matching dated schedules under the signed working-day/calendar-day/roster-shift convention. Working-day conversion excludes approved holidays. Encashment and separation/estate cases retain actual valuation/action. Published historical baseline, previous portal commitments and reviewed pending cases are all counted, preventing duplicate leave/encashment. |
| Exceptional Recreation cash-out | Signed Chief Secretary direction and Salary Unit valuation against an existing certified Recreation account. Reviewed pending determination holds credit against ordinary applications/corrections. Final grant debits it once; no new annual entitlement and no ABA/payment generation. |
| Recreation separation benefit | Sourced cessation/pro-rata exit valuation and signed payable amount against actual certified Recreation credit. Can be entered by central HR for an inactive employee; preserves employee/Payroll ID, service, ledger and history. Misconduct is a recorded separation reason requiring determination, not automatic forfeiture of every accrued benefit. Long service/furlough retain their narrower statutory retirement definitions. |
| Attendance | Central HR assisted case with employee response/evidence and due-process/authority reference; explicit excused-paid or reviewed-unpaid outcome and dated pay. No automatic AWOL finding, dismissal, disciplinary action or service exclusion. |

## Approval, integrity and privacy

Division → HOD → independent HR verifier → applicable relevant Secretary/Minister → Chief Secretary remains the authoritative order. Final grant rechecks current officer account/capability, dated assignments, applicant/entering-officer self-approval restrictions, submitted placement, policy/service/calendar/roster evidence, determination, benefit availability and every preceding approval. No administrator final-grant bypass or HOD-to-Chief-Secretary delegation is introduced. Office changes still require explicit reassignment or a linked continuation with fresh approvals.

Every case has immutable application facts, evidence hashes, versioned determinations, ordered decisions, final personnel PDF and append-only follow-up events. A changed document invalidates a prepared determination. Changes to service exclusions, appointment/basis, schedules, policy or calendar invalidate the retained calculation; cancellation/continuation obtains fresh facts instead of rewriting history. Ledger posting, case effects, tasks, final PDF, approval and audit occur in one transaction. A failure rolls the whole grant back. Stable request/decision/task/continuation keys distinguish safe retries from changed payloads.

Case reasons, clinical/personnel facts, supporting files and PDF stay behind owner/central/scoped evidence access. Ordinary approvers see the route, HR determination summary and salary/benefit decision facts, without clinical files or reasons. Calendar entries remain generic approved absence; financial decisions, attendance determinations and amendments are excluded. Approved shortening changes the effective calendar end; approved cancellation removes effective absence without deleting the original grant.

Pending applications can be assisted for staff without their own submission access, using an actual authority reference. A continuation copies retained evidence, recalculates against current foundations and restarts every stage in the same transaction as releasing the old hold. If the replacement fails, the original remains pending with its hold/decisions intact.

## Amendments and remaining boundaries

The implemented amendment is **one governed cancellation or early-return shortening per original grant**, with independently verified facts, Salary Unit correction instruction, retained Secretary consent and fresh Chief Secretary approval. It reverses only cancelled/returned original ledger units, or releases the appropriate financial commitment; an LWOP amendment withdraws/replaces the service pause. Original grant, decisions and PDF remain unchanged. Old task closure and amendment reconciliation are recorded, not erased. Changes that add dates or change category need a separately approved replacement application; additional corrections after the recorded amendment require a further governed reconciliation rather than silently reusing the original grant.

Financial prior-history/unit/transition facts are frozen when first committed and cannot be reset to hide earlier payouts. A changed certified benefit service basis or historical baseline remains blocked for the governed reconciliation/cutover in Package 5. Service history, prior encashment/LSL treatment, furlough day convention and actual Salary Unit monetary valuation still require real signed sources. The portal does not calculate salary from guessed rates or claim payment occurred merely because leave was approved. Witness grant inconsistency and the fifth-pregnancy/mid-leave clause require explicit determinations. Temporary parental and non-permanent LWOP exclusions cannot be overridden by entering a reference.

Supported assisted periods are bounded to 731 calendar dates, pay schedules to 24 segments and evidence to twelve retained files (three PDF/PNG/JPEG files of at most 5 MB per upload). Longer study/exceptional cases need an approved segmented plan. File signatures/size limits are checked; practitioner authenticity, Gazette authority, appointment facts and evidence content require HR verification. Malware screening and operational retention/security controls remain rollout work.

Actual Payroll/Salary Unit exports, broad legacy cutover, reconciliation exchange and TechnologyOne handover are **Package 5**. Concurrent-user/load tests, pilot acceptance, backup/restore, operational notifications, scheduler activation and production readiness are **Package 6**. These local checks do not certify a live deployment or 2,000 simultaneous users.
