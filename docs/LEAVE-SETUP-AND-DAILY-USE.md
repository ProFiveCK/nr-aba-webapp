# Leave setup and daily use

Open **Leave → Settings → Leave setup** to see the policy in use and the staff still awaiting transfer. Publishing a policy defines the shared rules; **Staff transfer** applies them to each reviewed group and carries forward its recorded credits. New applications use the current policy approval workflow. Staff whose transfer or activation is incomplete are directed to HR setup. Earlier applications, certified transfer snapshots and PDFs are retained as history.

## Finish setup

1. **Policy & calendars:** reuse the current published policy, or prepare the authorised version with its effective dates. Maintain shared holidays and work schedules here.
2. **Match existing records:** review suggested old-to-new leave names and organisation matches. Choose **Review record matches**, then **Save reviewed matches**. Existing personnel records and logins are reused. After this adoption, new profiles created through Employees or Payroll import start in Government leave setup. Import updates keep existing staff on their current arrangement until their reviewed transfer; creation alone grants no balances or activation.
3. **Approvers & organisation:** choose the approval levels and nominate the responsible people. The last nominated level grants leave; no employee can approve their own application.
4. **Staff transfer:** select a department and up to 50 staff awaiting transfer. Import any missing personnel setup, check their stored balances and record Medical absences already taken. Choose **Review staff transfer**, then **Apply staff transfer** when the entire group is ready. A changed balance or source requires another review. Temporary staff transfer Medical and Special only; Contract staff explicitly recorded without leave entitlement remain outside leave transfer. Existing credits above a new annual allowance carry forward; future renewals follow the Government policy.
5. **Automatic balances:** select transferred staff, confirm the saved fortnightly payroll cycle and review the schedules. If existing credits ended at the last recorded payroll, select **Continue accrual after the last credited payroll**. This records the payroll boundary separately from the administrative transfer date: the next full fortnight includes eligible service between payroll and transfer, at the agreed opening-policy rate, without backdating policy publication or applications. Continuity is limited to the reviewed first schedules after initial staff transfer and rechecks the latest recorded payroll credit. Save the reviewed schedules together. Existing schedules are retained. The server’s Government scheduler must be enabled for due postings; this is shown on the screen. Saving a schedule does not immediately credit a balance.

The active system administrator with central HR authority can complete routine policy replacement, activation and schedule reviews they prepared. Other central HR officers need a different configuration reviewer. Leave decisions, private evidence review and ordinary balance certifications retain their existing authority checks.

## Daily work

- **My Leave:** your current balances and account, an application form and application history. Choose dates, leave type and Medical certificate route when relevant. Review the date and balance check before submitting. Expand **Leave movements — in & out** for your credits and deductions, or **How leave is calculated** for the calculation basis.
- **Approvals:** requests awaiting the nominated approver. Earlier requests appear only while they still need a decision or have recent history.
- **Staff balances → View account:** search staff or department, compare available Recreation, Medical and Special days, then open an account for credits, deductions, pending holds and running balances. Central HR can see all active staff; officers with balance access see only their assigned scope. Missing certified balances show a dash rather than a policy default. Previous service-year balances are marked for renewal review; future openings are excluded from current availability.
- **Employees → Open employee:** manage personnel details, policy and approvers. Officers with balance access see the same balance account here. **Balance & setup changes** contains preparation and correction tools for central HR.
- **Overview:** activity and departmental planning, with a direct link to Staff balances.
- **Reports:** approved current-policy leave by period, with a CSV download.

The account reconciles **credits − deductions = balance**, then **balance − pending holds = available days**. The certified opening is included in credits. Each entry has its effective date, recording date, reason and source reference. Running balances follow recording order separately for each leave type and service year, including across pages. A backdated correction retains both dates. Pending requests hold days; final approval posts a deduction; rejection or cancellation releases the hold. Approved amendments return days through separate entries.

For the confirmed Treasury payroll boundary of **7 October 2026**, the next posting is **21 October**, covering **8–21 October**. The **9 October** administrative transfer does not remove 8 October from credited service. A full uncapped first fortnight credits **0.769230 Recreation days** under the 20-day annual policy; partial credited service remains prorated. Medical and Special renew on service anniversaries. Scheduled runs post only due dates and are idempotent; a prior fortnight is never credited twice.

**More administration** in Settings contains original setup receipts, HR access and account imports. The duplicate earlier balance editor and calculation settings have been removed from daily navigation. The earlier application submission endpoint rejects new requests, including from staff awaiting transfer; historical reads, cancellations and decisions remain available. Advanced operational reviews and individual exceptions are available without being part of the normal setup sequence.
