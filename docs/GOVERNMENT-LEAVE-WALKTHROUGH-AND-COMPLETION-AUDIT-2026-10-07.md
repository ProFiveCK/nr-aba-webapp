# Government Leave functional completion and walkthrough

Date: 7 October 2026. Local review: http://localhost:8081/review/local.

The local feature build now includes the remaining workflow changes identified in the earlier audit. The follow-up workflow consolidation replaces competing employee views with one selected-employee workspace, searchable organisation management, and clearly separated policy scope. ## Functional work completed

| Area | Behaviour available locally |
| --- | --- |
| Policy versions | Edit, delete and restore unpublished drafts with revisions and audit history. Prepare an authorised replacement during existing coverage; a different central HR officer approves it. Append-only transitions retain original published dates, grants and PDFs. Changes covering completed grants or posted jobs are refused. |
| Policy transitions and jobs | A payroll fortnight spanning a policy change prorates each governing annual allowance, retains its payroll phase and posts once. The posting-date policy sets its cap. Publication and grant/job execution coordinate through database locks. |
| Preparation recovery | Unwanted activation, entitlement-job, opening, teacher/roster coverage and benefit-reconciliation drafts have recoverable Delete draft / Restore draft actions. Discarded drafts cannot be approved; stale revisions fail. Approved/certified records remain protected. |
| Appointment corrections | Prepare a sourced correction of appointment dates, category, teacher/intern classification, service credit and schedule. A different central HR officer approves against the exact original facts. Before/after history is retained; existing balances, holds and grants are preserved. Affected activation/job reviews and pending applications require fresh review. |
| Enterprise approvers | Settings → Organisation & approvers contains division, Head of Department, Chief Secretary, HR evidence verifier, relevant Secretary and Minister offices. Dated appointments and explicit account permission remain separate requirements. |
| Assisted cases | Calculations fingerprint the foundation records used for the actual case. An unrelated future policy does not invalidate an unchanged pending case; changes to its governing facts still do. |
| Amendments and financial history | Successive approved early returns and subsequent cancellation append effects, refund only remaining entitlement and preserve original grants. Payroll uses the latest effect without duplicating the original grant. Correct prior payout/service baselines through independently approved reconciliation, preserving commitments and earlier history. Financial commitment changes use cancellation and a new reviewed determination. |
| Teacher and roster cohorts | Independently reviewed dated coverage records certify the actual teacher/shift schedule and its conversion. Teacher common-leave activation uses Medical and Special rather than ordinary Recreation; teacher Recreation stays on its discretionary case route. Nonstandard single-shift Medical conversion requires its own independently approved rule. Readiness retains identity, offices, balances, training and Payroll checks. |
| Management and operations | Government activity, outstanding stages, aged pending applications, Salary acknowledgement counts and current certified balances appear on Overview and Report. Government absence exports use dated grant segments and the latest approved shortening/cancellation. Scoped reporting excludes clinical reasons, evidence and pay rates. |

## Walkthrough

1. **Settings → Policies.** The usage counts identify employees on existing arrangements, preparing for Government Leave, active and paused. Rules, Holidays and Work schedules each have one configuration owner. The existing Finance rules remain under an explicit disclosure because they still govern existing employees. Publishing Government rules does not convert employees or rewrite balances. Unpublished drafts support Edit/Delete/Restore; a published version uses Prepare replacement and independent approval. The local published version still has a source saying authority is pending; obtain the actual signed authority before a production rollout.
2. **Settings → Organisation & approvers.** Search all departments/divisions, browse ten departments per page, and open only the selected department. Existing names are retained. Leave approvers and statutory/HR offices have server-side search, filters and pagination. Nominated managers remain in employee records; dated officeholders govern the Government route. Appointments do not grant account permissions. HR access remains separately scoped.
3. **Employees → Manage → Leave arrangements.** Inspect the current workflow, nominated manager, existing eligibility and balances, effective Government policy, certified accounts and full approval route together. Stored balances are distinguished from existing rule defaults. Employee details retains Payroll identities, placement, contacts and sourced appointment corrections. Matching retained placement names are preselected for explicit verification, never assigned automatically.
4. **Employees → Manage → Prepare Government Leave.** Stay in the selected employee through identity/appointment, login/enrolment, service/balance certification, retained-leave reconciliation and independent activation/accrual. Applying enrolment stops existing submissions. Government submission remains unavailable until preparation and independent activation are complete: plan that cutover with HR. Current APIs require Government enrolment before preparing openings and migration plans, so those steps cannot truthfully be presented as fully completed before enrolment. Signed targets and reconciliation preserve split Medical, Special, historical applications and previous grants rather than automatically overwriting them. Delete/Restore remains available for unwanted preparations.
5. **Employees → Manage → Applications.** Employee-scoped Government applications, assisted entry and follow-up tasks live here. Assisted submission is available after Government enrolment. Required dated approval stages and independent reviews remain enforced, with the final Chief Secretary processing after any required Secretary/Minister decision. Amendments retain original grants, PDFs and correction history. A previously selected application is cleared when changing employee.
6. **Employees → Leave operations.** Cross-employee applications/follow-ups, activation/accrual and Payroll/handover are distinct sections. Retained Finance administration remains explicitly available for historical imports, reports and maintenance. Employee preparation starts in Manage, rather than another competing directory.
7. **Settings → Employee setup.** Payroll identities previews the actual TechOne export, retaining leading zeros and letters. Login preparation supports verified cohorts and lifecycle controls. A multi-person cohort cannot be concealed inside a single employee's preparation screen. Provisioning still requires portal administration; it does not grant approval-office permissions.
8. **Overview / Report / Settings → Readiness.** Government queues and balances remain distinct from historical Finance measures. Check the actual cohort, independent teacher/roster coverage, evidence, offices, training and receiving-system reconciliation. Readiness does not silently activate logins, leave or jobs. Payroll exports provide instructions and do not execute payments; local email and automatic scheduling remain disabled.

Latest consolidation verification: **440 backend tests across 49 suites passed, with no skips** on a fresh disposable PostgreSQL database. Frontend: **116 passed, one existing skip**; lint and production build passed. The independent reviewer exercised 60 departments and over 50 appointments, plus employee-scoped onboarding, application pagination and cap alerts. Browser checks verified restored placement, eligibility, managers and balances, policy usage, selected-employee preparation and desktop/mobile layout. No real employee was enrolled or activated by the consolidation checks. Earlier capacity/recovery results below remain historical evidence and were not repeated for this UI change.

That elapsed fixture time is not a production recovery target.
