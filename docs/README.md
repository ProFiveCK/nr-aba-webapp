## Users and sign-in emails

Administration → User Management lists people by the job they do. **Add user** and **Edit** start from an **Access** choice (for example Staff member, Leave approver, Treasury reviewer); **Advanced access** holds the role and individual capabilities for unusual combinations.

An account's sign-in email is changed with **Change…** beside it (or the row menu). The change signs the person out everywhere, cancels password-reset and activation links already sent, removes the Google link so it can be re-made on the new address, updates a linked staff record's contact email, and notifies both addresses. The reason is audited.

## Policies & Procedures

The **Policies & Procedures** app is the library of policies, procedures, guidelines and forms in force. Every signed-in account reads what is published, staff with a Leave-only login included (they reach it from **Switch app** in Leave). Nobody needs a tick to read.

Two ticks on the account page, under **Policies & Procedures**, cover the work behind it:

- **Write and edit drafts**: register a document (code, title, kind, category, owning department, review date), write it in the editor or bring it in with **Import from Word** (.docx; headings, lists, tables, bold and pictures come across, fonts and colours do not), and add pictures and files.
- **Approve and publish**: read a draft and put it into force, saying what it changes and when it is next due for review; withdraw a document, or restore one.

Publishing never overwrites: the version it replaces is kept and readers can open it from the history. A document that was ever published can be withdrawn but not deleted. Each save names the draft revision it started from, so two editors cannot overwrite each other, and nothing is published that changed after the publisher opened it.

Printed copies carry "Uncontrolled when printed" with the version and date.
